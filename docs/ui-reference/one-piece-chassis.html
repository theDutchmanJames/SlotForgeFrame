How it is put together

  • A new scale, 1/43, beside 1/32 and 1/24: smaller wheels and tyres, slimline motors, and the rules and checks sized to match.
  • A new kind of chassis: one piece. Choosing it in New (or in Car) swaps the drawn chassis and the separate pod for one menu, One piece (Outline, Shape, Fill, Features and Pod step aside while it is chosen), with its parts on the right: plate and side pans, guide post, front axle, motor bay, gear well, rear axle and body mounts. Body, Inserts, Wings and Print work as they do now.
  • Peter's 1:43 Chassis is where it starts: built in, in New and in the library, with every size taken from Peter's model. Untouched, it builds the very same solid as Peter's STL; a test in the app checks that it stays so. The bar above says whether it still is, and Back to Peter's undoes your changes.
  • Linked sizes move together: the wheelbase moves the rear axle, the gear well and the end of the plate; the motor moves its bay and the walls round it; the track of the eyes follows the axle width.
  • Checks as for the other chassis: walls and eyes thinner than your nozzle, a motor that does not fit its bay, a crown gear that hits the floor, an axle too low for the tyres, a guide too close to the front axle.
  • Credit: designed by Peter (SlotForum), included with the designer's permission.

Ideas beyond the brief

  • The one-piece kind works for any scale: the same parts make a 1/32 or 1/24 one-piece chassis, starting from Peter's proportions.
  • Other people's one-piece chassis can be added to the library the same way later.
